Star Literate

Star Literate support

Star Literate is a reading game for children of about three to five who know some letters and are starting on letter sounds. A friendly star leads them along a trail through the Zoo, the Farm, the Beach, under the sea and up to the moon, with a short game at each stop. This page covers the questions parents ask most. If yours is not here, write to support@meyricke.com.

Getting started

Open the app and hand it over. Your child does not need to read: the star says everything aloud, and every screen works by tapping and listening. The first time, the star plays a short welcome game and shows how each new game works before your child tries it.

Turn the volume up. The star is heard even with the ring switch on silent, and the game makes little sense without its voice.

How it chooses what to play

The app watches which sounds and words your child gets right and keeps most of the play just past what they can already do. It never tests them, never shows a score and never uses a buzzer: a wrong tap gets a cheerful hint, and the help grows until they get there. The letter sounds come in the order used by systematic phonics teaching in English schools, and each is put to work straight away in real words.

The grown-ups' page

Press and hold Grown-ups in the corner of the trail for three seconds, then answer the sum. From there you can see what your child has been learning, choose the look of the game, switch the star's rests on or off, replay the welcome game, and start their learning afresh.

Stopping play

Every few stops, when your child reaches a new place, the star gets sleepy and settles down for a rest: a natural moment to put the game down. Tapping the star wakes it. You can switch the rests off on the grown-ups' page.

Two children on one device

The app keeps one child's learning. A second child playing will move it on as if they were the first. The grown-ups' page has a separate test player for adults trying the games, which never changes what the app knows about your child.

Starting again

On the grown-ups' page, Start her learning afresh forgets everything the app has learned about what your child knows. It cannot be undone. Deleting the app does the same and removes everything else it keeps.

Your child's data

Nothing about your child leaves the device. The app never goes online, has no account, no advertising and no tracking, and asks for no permissions. So there is nothing for us to see, reset, recover or delete on your behalf. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Contact
