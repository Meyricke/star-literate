Star Literate

Privacy policy

Star Literate, made by Meyricke. Last updated: 16 November 2026

Star Literate is a reading game for young children. It is built so that nothing about your child ever leaves the phone or tablet it is played on. This page says exactly what that means.

What we collect

Nothing. The app never goes online: it makes no network requests at all, so there is nothing for it to send and nowhere for it to send it. There is no account and no sign-in, and the app does not ask for your child's name, age, photo, voice or anything else about them.

There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data. We never see how your child plays.

What the app keeps on the device

To meet your child where they are, the app remembers some things between games. All of it is stored on the device only:

  • What your child knows: which letter sounds, words and skills your child has shown they know, worked out from how they play. The app uses this to choose the next game. There is no score and no test.
  • Which games they have seen: whether the welcome game and each game's demonstration have played, how far along the trail they have come, how many times each little book has been read, and the stars they have collected.
  • A record of play: which games and rounds were played, which letters and pictures were tapped, and how long a round took. It records play only, never anything about your child beyond that, and is kept to a few months' worth.
  • Settings made on the grown-ups' page: the look of the game, whether the star takes a rest at each new place, and whether the grown-ups' test player is switched on (a separate player for adults trying the games, so testing never changes what the app knows about your child).

A grown-up can start your child's learning afresh from the grown-ups' page. Deleting the app removes everything it keeps. If the device is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, that backup includes the app's data in the same way as every other app's; it goes to your backup, never to us.

Permissions

None. The app does not use the microphone, the camera, location, contacts, photos or notifications, and never asks for them. The star's voice is recorded into the app; nothing is listened to.

Children

Star Literate is made for children, and it follows Apple's rules for apps in the Kids Category and the UK Information Commissioner's Children's Code (the Age Appropriate Design Code). In practice:

  • no personal information is collected from anyone, child or adult;
  • no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no links out of the app;
  • the grown-ups' page sits behind a parent gate: a long press, then a sum a young child cannot answer;
  • the app is private by default, because there is nothing in it that could be shared.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date. If the app ever needs to collect anything, this page will say so before the version that does it is released.
